**Original template** authored for content-agent-kit (MIT, same as this repo).
Style direction from the 2026 code-video trend survey (`yihui-dev/awesome-opus5-5-videos`) and the
caption/punch-in grammar of `kurbaitaev/ghost-editor` (MIT); no third-party code, markup, fonts or
imagery is vendored here.

## Why it exists

Nested frames fly toward the camera forever while three lines replace each other in the centre, ending on the reveal. The endless-zoom prompt is one of the most-shared code-video formats, and it is the only template here whose motion IS the metaphor for going deeper.

- `hook`
- `depth_1`
- `depth_2`
- `depth_3`
- `reveal`
