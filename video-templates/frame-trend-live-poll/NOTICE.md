# Attribution — frame-trend-live-poll

**Original template** authored for content-agent-kit (MIT, same as this repo).
Style direction from the 2026 code-video trend survey (`yihui-dev/awesome-opus5-5-videos`) and the
caption/punch-in grammar of `kurbaitaev/ghost-editor` (MIT); no third-party code, markup, fonts or
imagery is vendored here.

## Why it exists

Two options fight: their bars race and overshoot before settling on the split, percentages land, and viewer comments float up the side. Built to end a video on a question that earns comments — the engagement beat Vietnamese short-form leans on hardest.

## Slots

- `question`
- `option_a`
- `option_b`
- `pct_a`
- `vs`
- `cta`
- `comment_1`
- `comment_2`
- `comment_3`

Both compositions are emitted together by `scripts/video/lib/build-trend-templates.mjs`.
Edit that file, not the HTML.
