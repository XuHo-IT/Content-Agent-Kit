## What is this?

A set of "skills" you install into an AI assistant such as **Claude Code** or **Antigravity**.
Then you just say what you want — *"make a video about choosing a T-shirt size"*, *"which ad is
running out of steam?"*, *"show me how Grab finds customer insight, for my shop"* — and the AI
follows a ready-made process: one step at a time, telling you what it is doing, and **always
asking before it publishes anything or touches an ad account**.

Built for: online shops, small brands, marketers, creators, news channels — anyone who needs
steady content without a whole team. Tuned for Vietnamese (voices, fonts, retail calendar, ad
law), and works in English and 140+ other languages with the optional voices below.

## 🏆 Learn marketing from 8 brands

Each brand is known for doing **one** thing well. The kit takes the *method* behind it, asks you
one question at a time, and applies it to your brand — never copying anyone's slogan, music or
campaign.

| Learn from | Lesson | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| **Grab** | Finding insight, solving the customer's real problem | An insight line *"Customers want ___, but ___, because ___"* from real comments |
| **Biti's Hunter** | Positioning and storytelling | A positioning sentence, one emotional territory, a story series |
| **PNJ** | Turning insight into a campaign that sells | One big idea + something concrete to buy + the right occasion + a target in numbers |
| **Cocoon** | Building a brand and a community | Values with proof, community rituals, briefs for customer content |
| **Vinamilk** | Brand strategy and repositioning | What to keep, what to change, why, and changing it everywhere at once |
| **MoMo** | Growth and viral marketing | A growth funnel, a refer-a-friend loop, a seasonal mechanic |
| **Highlands Coffee** | CRM and retention | Customer segments, a journey per segment, repeat-purchase tracking |
| **Coca-Cola** | Cultural marketing and brand experience | A cultural moment, the brand's natural role in it, an experience customers take part in |

Say: *"Show me how **Highlands** keeps customers, for my bakery"* — or just name the problem,
*"people buy once and never come back"*, and the kit picks the lesson. [The method →](docs/32-bai-hoc-thuong-hieu.md)

## 🚀 Get started in 4 steps

1. **Prepare your machine** (once): Windows, macOS or Linux with Google Chrome, plus Node.js and
   FFmpeg. On Windows, open PowerShell and paste:
   ```
   winget install OpenJS.NodeJS.LTS Gyan.FFmpeg
   ```
   macOS: `brew install node ffmpeg`
2. **Download the kit**: the green **Code → Download ZIP** button at the top of this page, then unzip.
3. **Install the skills** into your AI assistant — one of:
   - in Claude Code, type `/plugin marketplace add XuHo-IT/Content-Agent-Kit`
     then `/plugin install content-agent-kit@content-agent-kit`; **or**
   - open a terminal in the kit folder and run `node scripts/install-skills.mjs --kit --global`.
   - Antigravity: just open the kit folder, nothing to install.
4. **Restart the assistant and say** what you want (see the table below). Not sure where to
   start? Say **"start"** — the `creator-start` skill asks what you have and takes you there.

Every step in detail, how to check the install worked, and troubleshooting:
**[Quick start](docs/29-bat-dau-nhanh.md)**.

## 💬 How do I use it? Just say it

| You want | Say |
|---|---|
| A content machine for your industry | *"Build me a content agent for **handmade cosmetics**"* |
| What is hot today | *"What's hot about **electric scooters** today?"* |
| A competitor check | *"Analyse the channel **@competitor-channel** for me"* |
| A post every day | *"Make today's content"* |
| One video | *"Make a 60-second video on **choosing sunscreen**"* |
| A story video | *"Make a **brand story** video for my shop, with a turning point and a real customer quote"* |
| Edit your own footage | *"Cut the silences and add captions to **my-video.mp4**, **pop** style"* |
| Learn from a big brand | *"Plan a Lunar New Year campaign the **PNJ** way for my jewellery shop"* |
| An English version for international viewers | *"Redo this video **in English**, with a Google TTS voice"* |
| Post to Zalo, Shopee, TikTok Shop | *"Make an **upload pack** for Zalo and Shopee Video"* |
| See what you have spent | *"How much have I spent on AI video this month?"* |
| Photoreal AI footage | *"Make the opening shot with **Higgsfield**, quote me the price first"* |
| An ad policy check | *"Check these ads against policy"* (paste them) |
| Ad monitoring | *"Which ads are tiring?"* (send the Ads Manager export) |
| Comment replies | *"Sort yesterday's ad comments"* (send the comments file) |
| A sale plan | *"Plan Q1 ads with a 60 million VND budget"* |

### While the AI works you will see

1. One sentence up front: what it will do, how long it takes, what you need to have ready.
2. Each step announced: *"Step 2/5 — checking the script…"*.
3. **One question at a time** (paste numbers, pick a line, approve a draft).
4. **A stop to ask** before publishing, changing a live ad or hiding a comment.
5. At the end: where the result is and what to do next.

## 💰 Does it cost anything?

**✅ Free:** video with 135 frame types, 16 palettes and sound effects · a voice that runs on your
machine (OmniVoice) · trending topics, YouTube and news-site analysis · silence cutting, captions,
covers · the whole Facebook ads toolkit (it runs on exports) · the 8 marketing lessons.

**💳 Optional paid services — pick by the job.** None is required; add a key to `.env` when you
want one.

| Job | Providers | Good for | Payment |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🎙️ Vietnamese voices | **Vbee** · **FPT AI** · **Viettel AI** | Northern/Central/Southern accents, domestic channels | VND, Vietnamese providers |
| 🌏 Voices in many languages | **ElevenLabs** · **Google Cloud TTS** (50+ languages) · **Azure Speech** (140+ languages) | international channels; English, Japanese, Thai, Korean… versions | international card; Google and Azure have free tiers |
| 🎥 Photoreal AI footage | **Google Veo** · **fal.ai** (Kling, Hailuo, Seedance, Wan) · **Higgsfield** (Kling 3.0, Seedance 2.0, Hailuo 2.3) | shots a template cannot draw: food, streets, products | international card; **quoted first, stops at your spending ceiling** |
| 📤 Automatic posting | **Make.com** (free plan available) · **Ayrshare** | TikTok, Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, Threads, LinkedIn… without building a scenario | international card |
| 📊 Social media data | **Apify** | TikTok, Instagram, Facebook trends | international card |

You still need the AI assistant itself (Claude Code or Antigravity) on that provider's plan. The
kit's paid parts always **quote the price first** and only run once you add the key to `.env`.
Every paid call is logged; set a **monthly spending ceiling** and the kit stops before crossing it. Check your keys work (free): *"check my paid keys"*.

## ❓ FAQ

<details>
<summary><b>Will the AI post or change my ads on its own?</b></summary>

No. Everything that cannot be undone — publishing, editing or pausing an ad, hiding a comment —
waits for your yes.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Will the videos look "AI-made"?</b></summary>

Palettes are chosen per industry; no violet-to-cyan gradients or glowing text. Captions and
cutting rhythm follow what currently performs.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Can I use other people's videos and articles?</b></summary>

The kit only takes *ideas and sourced facts* and always writes new text. Someone else's footage
is used only when its licence allows, always credited. Customer praise or letters in a story
video must be **real**, used with permission.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Does "learn from Grab, PNJ…" mean copying their campaigns?</b></summary>

No. The kit teaches the *method* (finding insight, positioning, retention…) and applies it to
your brand. It never uses their names, logos, music or slogans. What is said about these brands
is marked "needs verification" — to mention one in a post, you need a source.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Is customer data safe?</b></summary>

Customer files are only ever written out hashed, and only for customers who consented, as
Vietnam's Decree 13/2023 requires.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Does the ad policy check replace a lawyer?</b></summary>

No. It catches common mistakes and always says *"not legal advice"*.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Can the videos use my brand colours?</b></summary>

Say: *"Take the colours from **your-domain.com** for the videos"*.
</details>
